03 · GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How creator fees, claims, and Solana settlement work in the Swarms marketplace.

Read the full creator docs

Every time someone buys or sells one of your tokenized products, you earn 0.5% of the trade value, paid in SOL directly to the creator-fees vault.

Fees accrue automatically on-chain — there's nothing to invoice or reconcile. You just claim when you're ready.

Anytime. There's no lockup and no minimum balance. You can claim a single product or batch all unclaimed fees with the Claim All Fees button at the top of this page.

Most creators claim weekly or monthly to balance gas costs against compounding.

Each claim is a Solana transaction. Network fees are typically ~0.000005 SOL — well under a tenth of a cent. Swarms takes no platform fee on claims.

Either there have been no buy or sell trades on it since your last claim, or it isn't a tokenized product. Only Tokenized products earn ongoing creator fees.
